Reason N°2

His Bald Head Caused Temporary Blindness

CAUTION: shine level "supernova".

Arno is bald, and his head shines brighter than the moon. One time I looked straight at it and went blind for 30 minutes. The next day I rushed to the doctor, who stared at me gravely and said:

"Son, what you experienced was Xtrasee Syndrome."

I had never heard of it. Neither had medicine. Until now.

🩺 Case Study #0001: Xtrasee Syndrome

Cause
Direct eye contact with Arno's scalp, especially in good lighting, bad lighting, or any lighting.
Main symptom
Temporary blindness (avg. 30 minutes).
Secondary symptoms
Squinting, sudden need for sunglasses indoors, and the urge to say "my eyes!" like a cartoon character.
Treatment
Welding mask, eclipse glasses, or simply looking at his forehead instead (see Reason N°3).
Prognosis
Full recovery, but your dignity may not return.

Xtrasee Syndrome is completely real. if you have witnessed the effects of Xtrasee Syndrome, you should go get help from the doctor as quick as possible.
