Reason N°1

He's Belgian

Arno is from Belgium, and let's be honest: Belgians are a little odd. No offense, Charlotte, but it's true.

Exhibit A: the evidence

  • 🍟 Belgians invented fries, then let the French take the credit. That's not humility, that's a cry for help.
  • 🗣️ The country has three official languages, and all three are used to argue about which one to use.
  • 🏛️ Belgium once went 541 days without a government. Nobody noticed. The fries kept coming.
  • 🧇 Their idea of a balanced breakfast is a waffle with a second waffle on top.
  • 🍫 They are the world's chocolate experts and still found a way to be weird about mayonnaise.
